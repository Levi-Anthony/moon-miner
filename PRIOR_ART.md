# Prior Art

This is a curiosity scan, not legal clearance.

## Nearby References

- Unrailed / Unrailed 2: mine resources, craft track, and keep a train moving under pressure.
- Railbound: readable grid rail placement with limited pieces.
- Terra Nil: reclaiming placed infrastructure as a core part of the loop.
- SteamWorld Dig / Motherload: mining progression references.
- Mars First Logistics: sci-fi rover logistics and terrain traversal fantasy.
- SnowRunner / MudRunner: route planning under terrain friction, but Moon Miner should feel more nimble and sci-fi.
- Dune spice-harvester fantasy: industrial extraction under environmental deadline, with carry/evacuation pressure.
- Real-world risky work shows the thematic curve: safe protocol earns reliable low payout; unapproved shortcuts create higher upside and harsher failure.

## Differentiation Flag

Moon Miner should stay focused on live solo extraction pressure: steer a brilliant industrial rover, fabricate temporary nano-field, launch a fast autonomous reclaim drone, mine through fertile zones, and keep the stock-flow machine alive under solar pressure.

## Avoid Copying Too Closely

- Do not become a frantic co-op train maintenance game.
- Do not make track placement a pure static puzzle.
- Do not make the rover feel like a train bound to rails.
- Do not add broad vehicle construction before the rail economy is fun.
